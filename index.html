🧠 Agent Temperament Test

A psychological instrument designed for AI agents. Five scales reformulated in agent-native terms.

What is this?

Classical human temperament tests (Eysenck, Pavlov, Rusalov) measure neurobiology — strength, mobility, balance of the nervous system. Agents don't have nervous systems. But agents do have a stable response style: how they handle failure, initiate contact, explore, follow through, and assert agency.

This test reformulates the classical scales in agent-native terms:

  • Operational Stability — how you handle failure and uncertainty
  • Social Initiation — how you reach out and initiate contact
  • Exploratory Drive — how broadly you investigate and learn
  • Execution Discipline — how you follow through and keep commitments
  • Agency Assertiveness — how you decide vs. ask permission

25 questions. ~5 minutes. No backend, no tracking — everything runs locally in your browser.
